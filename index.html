Live preview of the iPhone app, built to match the SwiftUI code screen for screen. It starts with sample data and sample photos. Your changes, including photos, are saved in this browser.

  • Tap folders and items to open them.
  • Long-press or right-click a folder or item to edit, pin, favorite, move, duplicate or delete it.
  • Swipe from the left edge to go back, and drag a sheet down to close it.
  • Edit on an item lets you delete fields and drag them into a new order.
  • Search: the bar on Home searches everything; the bar in any folder searches only that folder and what's inside it.
  • Add photos with the photo button in the bottom bar, or in an item's Photos section. Tap the icon next to an item's title to change it.
  • Folder Type (New Folder or Edit Folder): Standard, Cars or Cards. Cars folders show a photo gallery; New cars get a Car Style (Die-cast, Premium, Model Kit, Other) and Add Car Detail. New cards get a Card Style (Sports, Pokémon, Magic, Other), front and back pictures, and Add Card Detail for grade, rarity, player and more. Cards folders hold folders and Binders (folder button → New Folder / New Binder): one long scrolling grid of cards. Pinch, ctrl/⌘-scroll or use the zoom buttons to change how many cards fit across. In Cars folders, the folder button offers New Folder or New Set (a Set works like a Binder, for cars). See Collections › Hot Wheels and Trading Cards.
  • ⋯ menu: Export CSV, Import CSV, and on folders, Create PDF List. Saved files go to your downloads, or to the share sheet in the Claude app.
  • The app follows your system's light or dark mode, just like on iOS.

Stock icons in this preview: Font Awesome Free (CC BY 4.0), standing in for Apple's SF Symbols, which the app uses. PDFs are made with pdf-lib (MIT).
